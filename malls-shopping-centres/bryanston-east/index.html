Common questions

What are the trading hours?

Most centres in Sandton trade from around 09:00 to 19:00, later on Fridays. Individual stores vary, so check for the one you want specifically.

Is parking free?

Most offer free customer parking with a time limit. The number plate goes into a system at the boom gate, so note it down.

Which stores are open today?

Store hours differ from centre hours. Calling the store directly is more reliable than the centre's own listing.
