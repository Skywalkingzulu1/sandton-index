Common questions

Do you deliver and assemble?

Most furniture stores deliver within the Sandton area, and many will assemble on delivery. Confirm the delivery fee before you order.

Can I see the item before buying?

Showing stock in person is worth the trip for a large purchase, as finishes and sizes vary more than catalogue photos suggest.

Do you offer finance?

Many retailers in this category offer monthly instalments. Ask about the terms and the total cost, not just the monthly amount.
