Common questions

Can clothes be altered on site?

Many stores offer alterations. Ask before you buy how long a hem or sleeve takes, and whether the fitting can be done while you wait.

Do you stock plus and petite sizes?

Stock varies by brand. It is worth calling ahead about a specific size rather than making a trip across town.

Can I return an item without a receipt?

South African consumer law requires certain items to be accepted back within six months with a valid proof of purchase. Keep your slip.
