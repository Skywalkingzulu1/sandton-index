Common questions

Do I need to be home?

Not for most services, as long as there is safe access. Agree key or alarm arrangements in advance.

How is the price calculated?

Often by the hour or by area. A written quote before work starts is worth asking for on anything larger.

Are you insured and bonded?

For work in your home, this matters. Ask for the certificate if you are having electrical, gas or structural work done.
